What the characters are (meaning only, never draw text): "startup-os" gives a startup founder a small AI team. The team is real inside the product: a lead coordinator that splits off (spawns) small helper agents for each job. The helpers do the PREPARATION work — research, a health check of the business, a debate that challenges the founder's plan, rehearsing a customer meeting by playing the customer, drafting follow-ups. They never act in the outside world on their own: for anything outward-facing they hand the finished work to the founder and pause, waiting for the founder to decide. The founder is never drawn; the characters face the viewer, who is the founder.

Style (all three images):
- Matte clay / plasticine 3D, hand-made tactile feel: soft rounded forms, visible gentle fingerprints and tool marks, slightly imperfect edges, studio-photographed miniature on a tabletop, soft key light with gentle shadows, shallow depth of field.
- Dark, premium, calm setting: deep navy to near-black background (#0d1426 to #060608), matte dark tabletop. Character colours muted and warm, never neon: body in warm off-white / pale stone clay; the lead coordinator has a warm amber clay accent (#e88d4f); helpers each carry ONE small coloured clay badge on the chest from this set: soft cyan #7fd6e0, periwinkle #7fa7e8, lavender #c9a8f0, gold #f0d27a, lime #b9e38a, rose #f0a6c8. Accents of soft mint #99d7bc allowed.
- Non-human, gender-neutral, friendly but composed (not babyish, not goofy): simple pebble or capsule bodies, two small calm dot eyes, no mouth or a tiny line mouth, short stubby arms, no legs or tiny feet. Distinctive silhouette, readable when small.
- Absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, screens with readable content, or humans.
